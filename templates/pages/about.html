Welcome to our corner of San Francisco! Founded by Eloise Raymond and co-owned by Cole Smith, our cafe was born out of a college hospitality assignment that sparked a lifelong dream. What started as a classroom project blossomed into a shared vision: to create a warm, welcoming space where everyone feels at home, safe, and valued.

As a proud LGBTQ+-friendly establishment, community is at the heart of everything we do. We believe a great cafe is more than just a place to grab a bite—it is a gathering space. That is why our walls feature rotating works by talented local artists, celebrating the incredible creativity of the Bay Area.

Whether you are stopping by for a morning pick-me-up, settling in with a book, or admiring the local artwork, we promise you delicious food, friendly service, and a cozy atmosphere where you can truly be yourself.

Come on in—there’s always a seat for you at our table.
