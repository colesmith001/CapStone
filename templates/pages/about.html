Our story

Rooted in a simple idea: everyone deserves a seat.

Founded by Eloise Raymond and co-owned by Cole Smith, our café began as a college hospitality assignment and grew into a shared dream: creating a warm, welcoming space where everyone feels safe, valued, and at home.

We believe a great café is more than a place to grab a bite. It is a gathering space, a neighborhood living room, and a place to connect. Our walls feature rotating works by talented local artists, celebrating the creativity of the Bay Area.

Come in for a morning pick-me-up, stay for the company. There is always a seat for you at our table.
